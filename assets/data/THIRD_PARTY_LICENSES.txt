DomainScope — third-party wordlist and data licences
====================================================

The tiered wordlists in this directory (wordlist-base.txt, wordlist-large.txt.gz,
wordlist-huge.txt.gz) are built by tools/build-wordlists.mjs from the permissively
licensed sources below and are a substantial derivative of them. Their licences
require that this notice travel with the data. See assets/data/README.md for how
each source is used and the pinned commit.

Locale packs (locale/<cc>.txt) and the curated core in assets/js/lib/wordlist.js
are original DomainScope work under the project's own MIT licence (see ../../LICENSE).
